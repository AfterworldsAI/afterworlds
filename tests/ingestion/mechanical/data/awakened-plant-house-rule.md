# Awakened Plant

**Source:** Afterworlds supplemental house rule, Owner-authored 6 October 2026.
**Status:** Draft content; not published, installed, or activated. This creature
template is not part of the SRD 5.2.1.

## Use with Awaken

When Awaken turns a natural plant into a Plant creature, the GM chooses the
best-fitting supported template: the SRD's Awakened Shrub or Awakened Tree, or
this supplemental Awakened Plant. Awakened Plant provides a non-combatant
option for a plant that does not need a combat-oriented stat block. The GM
judges whether this Small template fits the player's chosen plant.

Preserve the spell's other rules, including eligible targets, Intelligence 10,
the language granted by the caster, movement and senses, and its temporary
Charmed condition and subsequent attitude choice. An existing Beast or Plant
creature targeted by Awaken retains its own statistics except for the changes
the spell supplies; this template addresses natural plants becoming creatures.
The SRD's open GM adjudication remains represented in its source rule.

**False Appearance.** While the plant remains motionless, it is indistinguishable
from a normal plant.
