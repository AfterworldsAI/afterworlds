## Material findings

JSON paths below are zero-based indexes into `candidate-c2/c2a.json`, `c2b.json` or `c2c.json`. Source paths are under `docs/sources/community/dnd-5e-srd-markdown/`.

1. **P1 — timed effects use the wrong subject.** C2-B `creatures[47].entries[2].attack.hit_effect.duration.whose` is `attacker` for Cloud Giant Thundercloud; source `monsters-A-Z.md:3971` says the target's next turn. Hill Giant Trash Lob (`[112].entries[2]`, source `:9295`) and Giant Vulture Gouge (`[283].entries[1]`, `animals.md:3564`) show the same family. `derive_c2.py:194` hardcodes attacker even when the source says “its.” **Class:** semantic correctness defect. **Disposition:** candidate correction; preserve whose turn governs each effect, including start versus end.

2. **P1 — save targets lose visibility predicates.** All six save effects marked executable omit the source's “can see” requirement. Guardian Naga Poisonous Spittle (`creatures[105].entries[3].save.targets`, `monsters-A-Z.md:8745`), Satyr Mockery (`[177].entries[2]`, `:14732`) and Constrictor Snake Constrict (`[248].entries[1]`, `animals.md:997`) are examples. `derive_c2.py:271–282` consumes that wording without representing it. **Class:** semantic correctness/eligibility defect. **Disposition:** candidate correction plus a named visibility-validation prerequisite; uncertifiable visibility refuses rather than being assumed.

3. **P1 — Multiattack alternatives and executability are incomplete.** Guardian Naga loses its ability to replace *any* Bite with Poisonous Spittle (`[105].entries[1]`, source `monsters-A-Z.md:8741`). Dragon Turtle loses its Tail replacement (`[63].entries[1]`, `:5278`); Ice Devil and five lycanthrope blocks share the parsing gap. Conversely, nine Multiattacks are called executable while their optional replacements are conditional because the rollup ignores `with_resolved`. Young Brass Dragon (`[31].entries[0]`) admits Sleep Breath while the referenced entry remains untyped/conditional (source `:2578`, `:2584`). **Class:** composition/certification defect. **Disposition:** preserve full alternatives and certify the *selected* composition. An unsupported optional substitution must refuse that substitution, not erase a supported base composition or silently admit the substitution.

4. **P1 — indirect contributing conditions escape the rollup.** Invisible Stalker Wind Swipe (`[122].entries[3]`) is executable with `blocked_by=[]`, although the same block has an untyped Invisibility contributor (`[122].entries[1]`, `monsters-A-Z.md:10103`). Invisible changes attack Advantage and defensive Disadvantage under `rules-glossary.md:996`. A literal “attack roll” search cannot establish semantic independence. **Class:** certification/unsupported-contributor defect. **Disposition:** correct the dependency classification using actual contributing semantics; do not ignore an untyped condition merely because its immediate trait text lacks a roll keyword.

5. **P1 — parameterized profiles still lose fixed authority and entry anchors.** All five parameterized profiles lack the ordinary defensive profile, including fixed ability/save/size inputs. Avatar of Death loses its printed damage and condition immunities (`creatures[335]`, `magic-items.md:3006`); Animated Object loses its immunities (`[330]`, `spells.md:467`). Animated Object, Giant Insect and Draconic Spirit have `entries=[]`; hand-authored assessed attacks are not anchored like ordinary entries, although accepted action components exist. **Class:** completeness/authority defect. **Disposition:** preserve fixed facts independently of conditional formulas and use the existing accepted component grains for assessed entries. This is the omitted-source repair already approved, not a reason to drop those creatures.

6. **P1 — extraction limitations remain product exclusions.** Barbed Devil's ordinary alternate Multiattack becomes conditional because of its wording (`[14].entries[3]`, `monsters-A-Z.md:1173`). Adult Black Dragon's three Rend attacks are blocked by an optional Acid Arrow substitution (`[21].entries[2]`, `:1732`). Ghast/Ghoul fixed-DC paralysis riders remain untyped (`[81].entries[2]`, `[83].entries[2]`, sources `:6756`, `:6919`). Caster-referenced values on existing creature actions such as Fell Glare/Venomous Spew (`spells.md:2390`, `:2828`) are missing authority/state dependencies to assess; they do not themselves prove that executing those actions entails casting/upcasting a spell. **Class:** scope/boundary problem. **Disposition:** candidate correction within approved bounded shapes. Name genuinely new mechanics separately; keep missing inputs distinct from unsupported capability.

7. **P1 — a tool option loses its actual action.** C2-A `facts[224].fact.option_key` is `which_falls_apart_in_1_minute`. Source `equipment.md:712` instead defines “Assemble a Tiny item composed of scrap, which falls apart in 1 minute (DC 20).” The comma-based extractor loses the action and produces a misleading selector identity. **Class:** selector/source fidelity defect. **Disposition:** candidate correction; retain the complete option and qualification. Independent parsing matched 25 tool abilities and 34 DCs, but found this option mismatch.

8. **P2 — raw option metadata does not yet reuse the accepted native family faithfully.** All 34 C2-A `ability_check` payloads carry `option_key` *inside* the fact. The current accepted `fact_from_payload` rejects all 34 with `MalformedFactPayloadError: ability_check payload carries extra ['option_key']`; all 38 weapon damage payloads validate. Native options have their own `ComponentOption.semantic_key` (`representation.py:10788`). This is not a demand that proposed new schema families parse under schema 18. It is a specific mismatch in the claimed reuse of an existing family. **Class:** representation/DTO mismatch. **Disposition:** explicitly map option identity to the native option grain without changing the accepted family, or disclose and review any actual family change. Prove correspondence before minting; raw JSON must not become an alternative authority system.

## Qualification and boundary residue

The ordinary arithmetic/sequencing versus declarative-fact boundary is compatible in principle with hand-authored adapters; it does not authorize runtime prose interpretation or model-authored values. The native review must include an explicit home for trust-relevant qualifications absent from the abbreviated raw facts. Examples: Light requires an Attack action on the actor's own turn (`equipment.md:76`); Graze damage can be increased only by increasing the attack ability modifier (`:100`); untrained armor causes the penalty, not trained armor (`:596`). The current Light/Graze/armor-penalty facts do not explicitly carry those clauses. Identify any precisely code-owned, source-bound procedure and its certification rather than silently treating retained prose as executable authority. Preserve the Cobbler's Tools next-Acrobatics Advantage qualification (`:659`) as an effect or explicit supported-operation boundary, not merely a selector label. This is **candidate/execution-contract clarification**, not a request for a general crafting or rules engine.

Minimum position/timing state belongs to the approved 15c scope. Its physical table organization is Claude's choice; persistent active effects must retain ADR-015 D8's subject/sheet ownership, independent creature state, revision checks and atomic delivered-turn application. An additive table must not establish a competing authoritative copy of conditions or a 15b/19b pending-sequence model.

The Vampire Familiar master relation is a real missing input (`monsters-A-Z.md:17503`), not evidence that summoner/controller equals master. The default refusal is appropriate **only for the affected Charmed determination**; it must not disable unrelated fixed mechanics. A small typed, validated master relation can be proposed as source-required state in the revision, keeping the exact semantic acceptance gate. A permanent product exclusion or broader relationship/lifecycle policy would require an Owner Decision. No new Owner Decision is needed to correct findings 1–8, and no such policy has been selected here.
