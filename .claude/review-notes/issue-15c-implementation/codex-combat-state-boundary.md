**Disposition: bounded implementation with concrete corrections. No blanket Owner approval is needed for a new table, or to reopen explicit actor-turn support. Spatial/elapsed-time input powers that exceed accepted source/context-constrained selection remain held pending an exact contract.**

Reviewed `boundary-input-matrix.md` against the full current #191 contract, ADR-0003, the 2b state/authority/hold contract and accepted turn-boundary representation. This is a boundary-over-patch classification, not approval of unspecified product policies. The accepted native C5 successor remains exact and independently reviewed; no source candidate change is requested.

- **Persistence placement — in-scope engineering.** #191 expressly includes minimum mechanical target, position and timing state. A bounded D&D-specific coordination table/document with revision is within that scope; its ordinary layout belongs to Claude. It must not duplicate sheet HP/resources, compete with authoritative persistent conditions/effects, write Story Bible canon, become a general encounter/initiative engine, or add a competing 15b persisted pending/sequence lifecycle. All affected state/bindings/holds and source provenance participate in the atomic delivered-turn transaction.
- **Distance representation — concrete correction.** A generic pairwise `within_reach`/`normal_range`/`long_range` value is not universal distance: the same pair differs by weapon/attack. It cannot alone establish Cleave's target-to-target five-foot constraint, area geometry, or the position resulting from Push. Bind predicates to exact admitted source thresholds, operation identities, subjects and contextual state, and retain enough information for each promised operation. Refuse absent/ambiguous/inconsistent predicates; do not treat an enum alone as geometric proof. Ordinary representation choices remain yours.
- **GameMaster selection — conditional scope, not arbitrary spatial authority.** #191 admits relevance and selection among code-admitted identities/options. Participant/story validation alone does not prove spatial eligibility or who belongs in an effect's area. Specify the operation-specific admitted spatial selectors, constraints, existing contextual inputs and auditable provenance that make a selection eligible. Do not silently give the model new power to establish arbitrary durable spatial truth merely because a value is an enum. If no existing accepted contract can establish the needed inputs, surface that exact power/input-origin question as an Owner Decision; hold only that dependent work.
- **Actor-turn edges — in scope in principle.** Explicit begin/end operations fit the approved actor-turn contract without managing initiative order. Code must validate legitimate distinct transitions, prevent duplicate edge consumption or manufactured budget resets/death saves, and derive triggers, counters and expiry from the exact source. A valid participant ID alone is not a legal transition. Never infer mechanical edges from narrative-turn count, or convert a round counter into elapsed hours.
- **Elapsed-time authority — concrete omission plus input-contract residue.** The proposed persisted fields omit the stable-recovery anchor/elapsed authority, although the approved lifecycle requires it. Supply the minimum explicit, auditable timing contract and source-derived recovery threshold. State exactly where elapsed-time inputs obtain authority. The issue does not grant an unspecified model power to assert arbitrary elapsed durations. If such new power is necessary, present that narrow policy before implementing it.
- **Cross-binding procedure — concrete compatibility check.** Each subject's own exact binding governs its own facts. Two individually valid certifications do not establish agreement on a shared procedure. Validate compatibility of the actual affected procedure/facts under both exact bindings and refuse an actual disagreement as incompatible. Do not select precedence or invent hybrid semantics; that would require Owner authority. No actual conflicting bindings were found in this bounded review, so there is no current abstract precedence question for the Owner.
- **Zero-HP classification — settled correction.** The matrix's "classification via document kind" contradicts #191. A pet/companion stored as a creature is not thereby a monster. Establish the source-defined treatment and record any per-subject GameMaster character-style exception; unresolved classification refuses. Do not reopen this accepted decision.
