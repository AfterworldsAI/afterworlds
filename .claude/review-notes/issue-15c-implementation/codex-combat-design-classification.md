# CRD Issue 15c — combat design classification

`combat-design.md` fits the approved subject/revision/delivery and explicit actor-edge boundaries in principle. This is design classification, not execution certification or final acceptance. The GameMaster spatial/timing input origins are now directly approved in `owner-context-decisions-2026-10-12.md` and #191; ordinary implementation may continue without another abstract visibility-policy question.

**Visibility is source-derived, but area membership alone is insufficient.** Bind the required spatial inputs to the observing/observed subjects, current operation and exact source thresholds. Cover, line-of-sight obstruction, illumination/obscuration and sense range must be adequate for the rule being consumed, rather than assuming two area-membership flags establish sight. Source-qualified Blindsight can see through Blinded/Darkness/Invisible within its range but not Total Cover (`rules-glossary.md:263–265`); Darkvision is not a universal answer to every obscuration. Area-effect membership must also preserve the source's point-of-origin/Total Cover rule (`:221`). Use approved bounded spatial facts, code-owned source qualifications and explicit refusal for missing/ambiguous inputs; do not invent a general spatial engine or arbitrary sight/numeric authority.

Preserve the other design invariants during implementation: recheck every actual subject and coordination revision/binding/hold; compare every contributing shared procedure; apply Turn/audit/effects atomically; invalidate movement-dependent facts even within linked operations; validate source-timed edges and consume effects once. The new treatment field must record a resolved per-subject classification and any approved exception. A generic outcome rule's `subject=monster` label, a table kind or an Animals template alone does not prove that the concrete subject is a monster; unresolved treatment refuses.

Unsupported contributing features remain explicit contextual refusals, not silently ignored modifiers or evidence of coverage. Source-category C6-r2 native preparation remains a separate semantic gate. No general feat/spell/rest-resource execution, new scheduler, publication, activation, shared migration or app launch is approved by this note.
