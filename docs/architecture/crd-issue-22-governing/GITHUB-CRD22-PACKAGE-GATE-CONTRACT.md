## Primary-source anchors and clock distinctions

The [IRS record-retention guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/how-long-should-i-keep-records) uses different periods and triggers for different records and circumstances. A 24-month operational receipt period cannot erase a separately applicable tax-supporting record obligation.

For applicable Canadian access requests, [OPC guidance](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/p_principle/principles/p_access/) states a normal 30-day response period from receipt, with limited extension grounds and notice. That is a legal-response clock, distinct from Afterworlds' 7-day export-production commitment and 7-day hosted download window.

Mexico's [current private-sector privacy law](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LFPDPPP.pdf), Articles 2 VIII and 31, defines business days and a 20-day ARCO determination period followed by 15 days to give effect to an accepted request, with justified extensions. Articles 10 and 24–25 address disposal/blocking and exceptions. Applicability and the exact record scope must be determined for the actual venture; these provisions are not a reason to restart service clocks or automatically retain a full Story.

Record original request receipt, verification and confirmation separately. Retries and administrator queues do not reset the approved service clock. An expiring download must not be misrepresented as a completed legal-rights process where further access/recourse obligations apply. Any required preservation must be explicitly scoped and disclosed in the retained-record receipt, with ordinary access blocked and no false claim of unrestricted final erasure.

## Exception record contract

A future exceptional-preservation record must contain only: exception ID and policy version; applicable jurisdiction/rule and decision reference; authority/approver; stable subject/request and precise record/field scope; permitted purpose and readers; start time and legal clock basis; end/release condition and review date; affected operations/deadlines; decision/notice references; and release/disposal evidence. It must exclude copied Story bodies, export bodies, credentials and arbitrary raw source payloads unless an actual reviewed legal requirement specifically necessitates the precise additional fields. Such an expansion requires recorded authority before effect.

Missing legal authority is not permission to keep extra data. Conversely, a legally required preservation cannot be bypassed to make a deletion receipt appear complete. Surface the exact unresolved scope and retain only what the accepted, applicable determination permits.
