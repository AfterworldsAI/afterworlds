# Accepted CRD Issue 22 Field and Store Retention Matrix

**Owner-accepted companion contract, revision 1; proposed records are not existing schema.** The CRD Issue 22 prerequisite package and clean `d3477eeaba42354e0048b69e4efec45b3ad0585f` source tree are the baseline. `CURRENT` names verified repository tables/fields; `PROPOSED` names a contract for future CRD22 or remote-platform stores, not tables that exist today. All retained IDs are still potentially personal. Anything not expressly allowed below is removed at the confirmed ownership boundary. The actual remote authentication, object/backup and processor stores await the D1 prerequisite; acceptance requires inventorying them before implementation, not assuming they are empty.

## Clocks, readers and proof

The export production clock starts with the **verified request** and ends with an accessible artifact within 7 calendar days. A completed hosted artifact expires 7 days after `available_at`; partial/failed staging expires within 24 hours of failure/abandonment. Story or account live erasure starts only at **verified, explicit scope confirmation** and must finish within 7 calendar days. Every backup has a 21-day rolling lifetime from original capture, including copies/restores; every backup affected by an erasure must be purged/expired by the **overall** 30-calendar-day limit from that confirmed request. Approval wait, retry, restart, copy and new attempt ID do not restart clocks. Final completion requires backup proof; an earlier active-data outcome remains visibly partial.

Minimized admin/request receipts expire 24 calendar months after their recorded action/request closure. Entitlement history persists while account active plus 24 calendar months after recorded account closure, unless a specifically determined financial duty applies to narrow fields. Story deletion does not close this clock. Content-free routine logs expire 30 days from recording. Suppression lasts until all relevant backup and replay resurrection risk ends, reviewed annually; minimum perpetual BYOK-rights evidence lasts while enforceable, reviewed annually. Legal response/preservation clocks are separate and must be determined for actual applicability. Use recorded UTC instants and defined calendar arithmetic; store policy version and original trigger times.

Reader abbreviations: **O** owning backend service; **S** authenticated owner/Sojourner or local BYOK owner receiving a filtered view; **A** authenticated Owner administrator under target authorization; **R** restricted retention/restore operator; **F** financial/legal reader only for a verified exception. None permits raw browser database access. Export paths enforce player-visible projection and never reveal hidden RPG values, credentials or unrelated subjects. Proof is an opaque receipt reference, per-class/Story count, timestamp, policy version and store result, not a retained sample of deleted content. Do not retain a content hash that permits inference of deleted content or unwanted cross-subject correlation. Use opaque operation/count evidence, or an approved restricted keyed proof where a digest is necessary.

## Exact accounting and perpetual-rights allowlist

**CURRENT event envelope:** `global_sequence` (replay order), stable `event_id`, `sojourner_id` (subject), `event_type`, nullable `turn_id`, nullable `idempotency_key`, `payload_json`, `created_at`. The existing typed payload parser requires `schema_version: 1`, rejects unknown fields, and uses business timestamps only as effect metadata. The only acceptable retained representation is the existing typed row if every field meets the privacy allowlist, or an **approved versioned entitlement-owner migration** that keeps ID/order/source, exact effect, parser/replay and idempotency equivalence. Never edit arbitrary JSON behind the trigger, retain a second accounting ledger or delete a retained event that makes replay wrong.

| Event kind | Required CURRENT typed fields to preserve as replay-valid, with allowed purpose | Free text/secret boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Hosted activation | `schema_version`, `plan`, `effective_at`, `external_source`, `external_reference_id` for access/source provenance. | Source fields are bounded codes/opaque references only. |
| Hosted deactivation | `schema_version`, `effective_at`, typed `reason`, `detail`. | `detail` is free text: content-free/bounded at creation or converted only through an approved versioned parser/migration; never silently dropped from v1 JSON. |
| Subscription credit grant | `schema_version`, exact `hosted_credit_delta` (4 decimal places), `period_start`, `external_source`, `external_reference_id`. | No invoice/customer narrative or processor secret. |
| Top-up credit grant | `schema_version`, exact `top_up_credit_delta`, `external_source`, `external_reference_id`. | No payment payload. |
| Credit deduction | `schema_version`, exact `hosted_credit_delta`, `top_up_credit_delta`, envelope `turn_id` for one-turn idempotency. | Opaque Turn ID only, never Turn text. |
| BYOK license activation | `schema_version`, `purchased_at`, `external_source`, `external_reference_id`. | Perpetual right evidence must remain resolvable independently of deleted cloud profile. |
| Cloud Services activation/lapse | Activation: `schema_version`, `expires_at`, `external_source`, `external_reference_id`; lapse: `schema_version`, `effective_at`, typed `reason`, `detail`. | Lapse `detail` has the same free-text restriction. |
| Manual adjustment | `schema_version`, exact signed `hosted_credit_delta` and/or `top_up_credit_delta`, bounded safe `reason`, trusted stable `adjusted_by`. | No profile, Story, arbitrary ticket/incident text or credentials. |

During approved retained lifetime O can replay, A can inspect safe source-bound history, S can inspect own safe history, F only narrow verified duty. Expire event/projection under entitlement owner after active account plus 24 months closed, unless exact financial fields/duty are separately approved. Preserve minimum perpetual-rights evidence while enforceable: right/license ID, opaque subject-link token, right kind/status, purchase/activation time, source/reference ID and verification metadata; no cloud profile, Story, key or raw credential. The exact rights-owner mapping is a prerequisite, not an existing table claim.

**Replay-equivalence proof:** at a fixed event boundary, snapshot the parsed ordered event stream and authoritative projection. Include negative balances, included-first spending, Cloud lapse/expiry and BYOK independence. Apply Story deletion and account closure/minimization. Reparse with the entitlement owner's versioned parser, replay `global_sequence` and compare balances/access, stable event/source/idempotency references and event effects before/after throughout retention. Closure gating is separate from historical projection. Inspect stored rows/views for narrative/profile/secret leakage. On eventual expiry, prove restored/replayed data cannot reactivate a closed subject and the perpetual right still verifies. If v1 `payload_json` cannot meet both privacy and replay, accept a versioned owner contract before implementation; do not make a lossy redaction or parallel ledger.

A field not needed by the selected identity mechanism need not be collected. Any additional collected field needs an explicit owner, purpose, export/deletion classification and retention rule before affected implementation. This is not permission for arbitrary profile JSON or hidden copies of authentication data.

No remote credential-store record for raw BYOK keys is admitted. Hosted service-owned credentials and identity-provider authentication secrets require the remote prerequisite's actual secret-custody contract, restricted readers and lifecycle; they cannot become user data exports or generic logs. CRD Issue 23 must enumerate any future payment/customer references needed for real commercial obligations; their current schema is not invented by this matrix.

Live-store proof must cover content remnants in SQLite WAL/journals and reusable pages, vector persistence, replicas and managed caches as well as logical row/chunk deletion. Use supported reviewed disposal/compaction or cryptographic controls appropriate to the actual storage, without deleting other accounts' data. Classify each actual copy in the inventory; calling a live journal a backup cannot silently extend its approved live-data deadline. Unavailable/unverified disposal remains a failure.
