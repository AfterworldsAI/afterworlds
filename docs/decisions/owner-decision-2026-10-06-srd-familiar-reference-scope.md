# Owner Decision - SRD familiar reference scope

Recorded 6 October 2026 for CRD Issues 5c and 5d (#132, #137), inventory
references 60 and 48. This settles the supported source boundary, not acceptance
of an exact candidate or recording of either reference resolution.

## Owner instruction

> 60 (and probably 48, too): Any animal of CR = 0 can be called as a familiar. This includes things like dogs smaller than a wolf, badgers, flamingoes, goats, toads, foxes, red pandas, hedgehogs, armadillos, and jackals to name a few. If a comprehensive list is necessary, go through the list of animals and add any with CR = 0 to the Find Familiar list. For now, we will limit players to animals appearing in the SRD, ergo no flamingo familiars. A flamingo familiar is not against the rules; in fact, it's entirely appropriate for a certain sort of player and wizard character to have one--but we are limited to animals with stat blocs appearing in the SRD. A future AW house rule may expand the Find Familiar options to other CR = 0 creatures not in the SRD. Players can always make their own house rule if they want a very specific familiar and are willing to provide the stat bloc for it.

## Decision and source interpretation

- Find Familiar's supported normal forms include all existing SRD 5.2.1 Beast
  stat blocks with CR 0, not just its eleven expressly named examples.
- This is the current Afterworlds supported-source boundary. Preserve the SRD's
  broader permission to choose another CR 0 Beast; do not amend its source text
  to claim non-SRD forms are forbidden by the game rules.
- Do not invent missing animal stat blocks, reskin an existing one as a newly
  canonical animal, or infer CR from animal size or ordinary zoology. Potential
  future house-rule expansion is not implementation scope for this candidate.
- The same normal-form set applies to Pact of the Chain: its source expressly
  permits normal Find Familiar forms or seven named special forms. Preserve
  Imp, Pseudodragon, Quasit, Skeleton, Sphinx of Wonder, Sprite, and Venomous
  Snake as additional choices without imposing CR 0 or Beast requirements on
  those source-authorized exceptions.

Official PDF p.130 and qualified spells.md:2290 state the ordinary-form rule.
Official PDF p.74 and qualified classes.md:9126-9130 establish Pact of the
Chain's inheritance and special forms. The source requires Beast type for the
general CR 0 permission; CR 0 Plants, Constructs, Fiends, or Humanoids do not
qualify through that permission. The familiar's resulting Celestial, Fey, or
Fiend type is separate from the eligibility of the chosen animal form.

## Verified finite SRD inventory

The [inventory](../../.claude/review-notes/issue-5d-srd-familiar-cr0-inventory-2026-10-06.json)
lists 25 qualifying source stat blocks, all with existing accepted creature
records: 24 in Animals and Giant Fly in Magic Items. Giant Fly's printed block
on PDF p.222 was visually checked: it is a Large Beast, CR 0. Neither the
spell's source rule nor this Owner boundary imposes a size limit or confines
eligible stat blocks to the Animals chapter. Preserve Giant Fly's existing
ownership under Figurine of Wondrous Power; do not move it to an Animals record.

The eleven named normal forms are Bat, Cat, Frog, Hawk, Lizard, Octopus, Owl,
Rat, Raven, Spider, and Weasel. The fourteen additional supported forms are
Baboon, Badger, Crab, Deer, Eagle, Giant Fire Beetle, Giant Fly, Goat, Hyena,
Jackal, Piranha, Scorpion, Seahorse, and Vulture. Pact of the Chain consequently
has these 25 normal forms plus its seven distinct special forms, 32 in total.

The inventory was derived from all qualified source Markdown files using
stat-block headings, Beast type, and exact CR 0, then matched to existing
accepted creature records. Quantity, name similarity, prose animal mentions,
and non-Beast CR 0 blocks did not establish eligibility. This is source and
destination inventory verification, not native candidate or reader verification.
